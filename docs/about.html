About Gasiin

Feeding the herds that feed the region

Gasiin Enterprise is an agribusiness built around one idea: livestock can only be as productive as the feed it gets.

Vision

To become Somalia’s leading provider of high-performance, affordable animal nutrition, transforming livestock productivity and drought resilience across the region.

Mission

To design and make precise, species-specific feed for dairy, poultry and sheep-fattening enterprises, raising herd yield and farm profitability at an accessible cost.

The name

What “Gasiin” means

In Somali, gasiin means feed, or food, for animals and for people alike.

The name describes the whole chain we work in: fodder grown on Somali land becomes feed, feed becomes healthy livestock, and livestock becomes food and income for families and export markets.

The problem

A feed gap that costs lives and livelihoods

Livestock is the backbone of Somalia’s economy: more than 56 million animals, around 55% of GDP and about 80% of export earnings. Yet the country produces far less feed than its herds need. AU-IBAR estimates national demand at 2.7 million tonnes a year against domestic production of 1.2 million tonnes, a gap of 1.5 million tonnes every year.

When the rains fail, that gap turns deadly. More than 10 million animals were lost in the past decade, including 6.4 million in the 2016–17 drought and 3.8 million in 2021–22. Commercial farmers who want to grow are left choosing between costly imported concentrates and seasonal grass of poor nutritional value.

Source: African Union Inter-African Bureau for Animal Resources (AU-IBAR).

How we work

From field to feed bag

Gasiin began with a livestock-fattening pilot in Arabsiyo district and is now building the full feed supply chain:

  1. Our own farmland is the base for high-yield forage grasses and feed grains such as maize, and sets the quality benchmark for everyone who supplies us.
  2. Contract farmers and local millers will add year-round volume: forage, stover and oilseed cakes from farms in rain-fed, irrigated and riverine areas, plus wheat bran and pollard from Somali flour mills.
  3. Certified regional suppliers in Kenya and the East African Community will provide high-protein meals, vitamin and mineral premixes and feed-safety additives.
  4. Processing and storage: we are equipping a processing hub with balers, forage choppers, silage wrapping, hammer mills and mixers, with climate-protected storage.

Gasiin’s office is in Shacab Area, Hargeisa.
